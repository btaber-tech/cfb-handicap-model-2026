Market numbers are shown for reference/line-shopping only — the model-vs-market gap is shown on every game as a % of the market's own line (a 12pt gap matters less on a 40pt favorite than a 6pt gap does on a 1.5pt line) and highlighted gold at 25%+, or 3+ raw points for near-even lines where a percentage stops being meaningful. Either way it's a "look closer" flag, not a bet signal by itself (no backtested edge tracks with gap size). Model: margin = avg(SP+, FPI, bottom-up 2026 model, Steele Power Poll) + tiered home-field advantage, win probability from a normal CDF (σ=18.4). Steele's Power Poll (his own blend of 9 talent-rating systems) is rescaled from an ordinal 1-138 rank to a point scale comparable to SP+/FPI before averaging in -- included at equal weight to the other three sources, deliberately weighted above Athlon's national rank (comparison-only, not blended in). HFA/sigma recalibrated against 2,210 FBS games (2023-2025): +2.9 at a true home site, +1.8 at a "neutral" site (most still favor one side), +6.0 for altitude-market home teams (Air Force, BYU, Colorado, Colorado State, New Mexico, Utah, Wyoming). From week 3 the margin also blends in each team's opponent-adjusted 2026 results (ramping weight). Totals are a calibrated offense/defense matchup average: preseason SP+ off/def blended with each team's opponent-adjusted 2026 points scored/allowed (more games played = more weight). More accurate than preseason-only in a 2023-25 replay, but still well behind the market's total and no over/under edge -- context only.

W-L and ATS columns: each team's season-to-date record before this week, listed in matchup order (hover for the team). W-L includes games vs. FCS opponents; ATS counts games with a line, graded against the median closing spread across books (W-L-push). Context only -- not a model input.
