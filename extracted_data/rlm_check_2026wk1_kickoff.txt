METHOD: for each game, compare VegasInsider's own OPEN -> CONSENSUS
spread move against the bets% ticket majority. Real RLM = the line moving
AGAINST the side the majority of bettors are on. Games with a near-
unanimous (85%+) bets split are marked low-confidence — on 25+ point
blowouts, the line more likely moves on power-rating/model updates than
retail bet pressure. This mirrors the methodology in
build_public_betting_report.py / cfb-handicap-model-2026 memory.

with a mostly-frozen market (per line_movement report); today's check is
same-day/next-day for the earliest games, when real money and injury/
depth-chart news move lines the most.
