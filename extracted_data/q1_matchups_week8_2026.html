Which teams are most likely to be up big after the 1st quarter. Each team's projected Q1 points come from its offense against the opponent's defense (season scoring and Q1 scoring, with red-zone TD rates shown for context). The headline number is the chance the stronger side leads by 7+, 10+ or 14+ after one quarter, with the fair odds that chance implies.

Each stat is a matchup: team offense vs. opponent defense, minus the same for the other side. Season scoring beats Q1-only scoring because it is built on four times as many points. Red-zone TD rate is weak and adds nothing once scoring is in.

How the model is weighted

Weights are fitted to 827 games (2025 and 2026, each week using only earlier games). The red-zone weight fitted slightly negative, which is noise, so it is set to zero and red-zone rates are shown on the cards as context only.

Lead chances come from a logistic curve on the projected margin, fitted per lead size and checked on a held-out season. Projected margin picks out 14+ leads about 77% of the time (50% is a coin flip). A team's own past 14+ blowouts this season were a weaker guide (69%), so they are not used.

The market spread is still the better single predictor. When this board and the spread disagree on the side, the spread has been right, so treat a "Disagrees" game as a warning rather than an edge.
