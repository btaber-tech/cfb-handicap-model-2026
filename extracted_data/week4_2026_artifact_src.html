Market numbers are shown for reference/line-shopping only — the model-vs-market gap is shown on every game as a % of the market's own line (a 12pt gap matters less on a 40pt favorite than a 6pt gap does on a 1.5pt line) and highlighted gold at 25%+, or 3+ raw points for near-even lines where a percentage stops being meaningful. Either way it's a "look closer" flag, not a bet signal by itself (no backtested edge tracks with gap size).
